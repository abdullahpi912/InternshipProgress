What are Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K), and why do they matter?

Nitrogen (N) supports rapid leafy vegetative expansion; Phosphorus (P) accelerates root branching, seedling vigor, and flower/fruit set; Potassium (K) regulates cellular osmotic pressure, water transport, and disease resilience.

How does soil pH affect plant nutrient uptake and crop yield?

Soil pH dictates chemical mineral solubility. Most agricultural crops thrive between pH 6.0 and 7.2. Acidic soils (< 5.5) or alkaline soils (> 8.0) lock essential macronutrients, rendering fertilizers ineffective regardless of application quantity.

What is the difference between Crop Selection and Fertilizer Dosage advisory?

Crop Advisory identifies the single highest-yielding crop species for your unamended field parameters. Fertilizer Advisory calculates the precise NPK deficiency dosage (e.g., Urea, DAP, NPK mixtures) needed to nurture a specific target crop in your soil.

How does AgriSense account for regional weather and seasonal rainfall variations?

The AI classification model evaluates field soil nutrients together with environmental vectors (seasonal rainfall in mm, average temperature in °C, and relative humidity %) to prevent recommending drought-vulnerable or moisture-sensitive crops.

Can I log and track historical soil tests across multiple farm plots?

Yes! Creating a free farm profile allows you to store historical field logs, track soil nutrient restoration over multiple harvests, and rerun ML recommendations whenever new soil tests are completed.
